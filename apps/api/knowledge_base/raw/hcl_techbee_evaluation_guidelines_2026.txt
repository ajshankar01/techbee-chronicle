# HCL TECHBEE INTERVIEW EVALUATION POLICY & CANDIDATE BENCHMARKS (2026)
Document ID: HCL-TB-RUBRIC-2026-V1
Classification: Confidential - HR & Evaluator Guidance

---

## SECTION 1: PROGRAM MISSION & CANDIDATE PROFILE
HCL TechBee is an early-career work-integrated learning program designed for 12th-standard high school graduates.
Evaluators must measure candidate potential based on curiosity, communication clarity, problem-solving, and adaptability.
Since candidates are 17-19 years old, evaluation must reward structured thought and logical coherence over advanced corporate jargon.

---

## SECTION 2: SEGMENT 1 - ORAL COMMUNICATION & SELF-INTRODUCTION
### 2.1 Cadence and Fluency Standards
- Optimal Speaking Rate: 110 to 140 words per minute (WPM).
- Below 90 WPM: Marked as hesitant; deduct 10 points in communication cadence.
- Above 160 WPM: Marked as rushed/nervous; deduct 5 points in articulation.
- Filler Word Tolerance: Up to 3 filler words (e.g., 'um', 'like', 'uh', 'you know') is acceptable. More than 6 filler words results in a 15-point deduction in fluency.

### 2.2 Core Content Requirements
The candidate's introduction must cover three specific areas:
1. Academic background and journey so far.
2. Core technology passions (e.g., Python, Web development, AI, Cloud, Cyber Security).
3. Motivation for joining HCL TechBee rather than conventional college programs.

---

## SECTION 3: SEGMENT 2 - COGNITIVE & LOGICAL ABILITY
### 3.1 Scoring Benchmarks
- Maximum Score: 100 points across 10 questions (10 points per question).
- Domain Breakdown:
  - Numerical Reasoning: 3 questions (Target: minimum 2 correct).
  - Logical Deduction: 4 questions (Target: minimum 3 correct).
  - Pattern Recognition: 3 questions (Target: minimum 2 correct).
- Overall Cutoff for Freshers: Minimum 60% total cognitive score. Candidates scoring 80%+ qualify for High-Performer fast-track streaming.

---

## SECTION 4: SEGMENT 3 - PSYCHOMETRIC & WORKPLACE CULTURE FIT
### 4.1 Scoring Structure & Reverse-Scoring
- Total Questions: 15 questions evaluated on a 1-5 Likert scale (Strongly Disagree=1 to Strongly Agree=5).
- Reverse-Scoring Guardrail: Exactly 7 questions are phrased negatively. For these 7 questions, positive alignment is scored in reverse: Score = (6 - candidate_selection).
- Critical Competencies:
  1. Adaptability to shift timings and corporate discipline.
  2. Team collaboration and receptive attitude toward constructive feedback.
  3. Resilience under high academic and project workloads.
- Red Flags: A score below 2.5 on resilience or coachability requires secondary HR review before offering a TechBee bond contract.

---

## SECTION 5: SEGMENT 4 - PROFESSIONAL EMAIL WRITING
### 5.1 Rubric and Deduction Rules
- Minimum Word Count: 50 words; Maximum Word Count: 180 words.
- Mandatory Salutation: Must open with professional salutation (e.g., 'Dear Hiring Manager', 'Hello Team', 'Respected Sir/Madam'). Missing salutation = 15-point penalty.
- Professional Sign-off: Must conclude with formal closing (e.g., 'Warm regards', 'Sincerely', 'Best regards', followed by candidate name). Missing sign-off = 10-point penalty.
- Subject Line: Must be concise and relevant to the interview prompt.
- Tone: Must be courteous, solution-oriented, and free of SMS abbreviations (e.g., 'u', 'plz', 'thx'). Any informal SMS abbreviations incur an automatic 20-point deduction.

---

## SECTION 6: PROCTORING & INTEGRITY THRESHOLDS
- Maximum Allowed Tab Switches: 2 (Third tab switch flags the session for supervisory audit).
- Face Visibility: Candidate face must remain in frame for at least 85% of total session duration.
- Copy-Paste Detection: Segment 4 (Email) prohibits clipboard paste. Detected paste events trigger immediate manual review.
